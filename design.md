# Design & UX Specification
## "The Lenny Growth Assistant"

---

## 1. Design Aesthetics & Rationale

- **Theme**: Dark Glassmorphism (`#090d16` canvas with slate blue cards `#0f172a` and glowing electric cyan borders `#38bdf8`).
- **Typography Hierarchy**:
  - **Headings**: `Outfit` (Bold, geometric, premium tech feel).
  - **Body Text**: `Inter` (Optimized for readability and long-form essays).
  - **Code & Artifacts**: `JetBrains Mono` (Clean developer aesthetics).
- **Visual Feedback & Micro-Animations**:
  - Hover glow effects on prompt chips and buttons.
  - Collapsible citation cards with smooth transition.
  - Streaming loading spinner with context status indicator.

### 2.1 Interaction States
1. **Empty State**: Displays curated high-leverage prompt chips for instant evaluation.
2. **Streaming / Processing State**: Displays active step badge ("Analyzing Lenny's transcripts and executing skill...").
3. **Artifact Split View**: Dynamically splits workspace 50/50 when an artifact is generated or clicked.

## 3. Security & Isolation Rationale for Artifacts

HTML artifacts generated by LLMs are treated as **untrusted user content**.
- **Defense in Depth**:
  1. **DOMPurify Sanitization**: Strips dangerous scripts, `onload` handlers, and `javascript:` URLs.
  2. **Iframe Sandboxing**: Renders inside `<iframe sandbox="allow-scripts">` blocking parent DOM access, cookie theft, or top-level navigation.
